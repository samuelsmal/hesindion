The Waffeneigenschaften page (Aventurische Rüstkammer p. 56) says weapons' Vor- und Nachteile "nur zum Tragen kommen, wenn du mit der Fokusregel spielst", and Optolith sources this weapon's advantage and disadvantage to the Rüstkammer (p. 83), not to the Regelwerk. The page also lists Aventurisches Götterwirken p. 233, which could print the Dornenspitze as part of the weapon itself — not checked (no access to the book). The app has no such Fokusregel and applies all three always (catalog ITEMTPL_19, WeaponFumbleExtras). The same holds for the Großschild's Waffenvorteil and -nachteil (ITEMTPL_29.GR2–GR4) and every other weapon's; this ruling may belong in rules/rulings.yaml.

Situations: kupperus-und-waffen 18.7, kupperus-und-waffen 18.5, kupperus-und-waffen 18.9, kupperus-und-waffen 18.10, kupperus-und-waffen 18.12, kupperus-und-waffen 18.13

- **a** Only with the Fokusregel, like every weapon's Waffenvorteil and -nachteil. *App:* a Fokusregel toggle (Ausrüstung, Stufe I); Dornenspitze, +1 TP and Betäubung only when it is on — *recommended*
- **b** The Rabenschnabel's are part of the weapon and always apply; other weapons' need the Fokusregel. *App:* as today for the Rabenschnabel; the toggle gates the rest
- **c** Always, for every weapon — the group plays with Waffeneigenschaften. *App:* no toggle; as today, and the missing Großschild lines are added

Why a: The Fokusregel page is explicit and the text comes from the Rüstkammer, the Fokusregel's book; a group that wants the lines turns it on, and the owner's house rule can make it the default.

### `shared` · kampfstil-techniques

Answer in [`rules/rulings.yaml:16`](./rules/rulings.yaml#L16)

**A Kampfstil lists combat techniques, sometimes narrowed to weapons. Does the style's effect apply only while fighting with one of them?**

Golgariten-Stil lists Hiebwaffen (only Rabenschnabel) and Schilde (only Großschild, only mounted). The style's own page does not say "only while fighting with", and the wiki's Kampfstil overview has no general rule text. The catalog today turns the style on only with a Rabenschnabel or a Großschild in hand, so a Golgarite with a Schwert gets AT +2, PA +2 instead of AT +4, PA +3. The same question comes up for every Kampfstil. Vinsalt-Stil (a defensive style) lists Armbrüste, which suggests at least that list says who can learn the style rather than when it applies.

Situations: reiterkampf 5.7, mehrfache-verteidigung V-cases on Vinsalt-Stil

- **a** Only while the hero fights with a listed technique (and weapon, where narrowed). *App:* as the catalog does today — *recommended*
- **b** Whenever the style's own conditions hold; the list only says which techniques can learn it. *App:* the style applies with any weapon
- **c** Decided per style, in each style's file. *App:* a field per Kampfstil

Why a: A style is a way of fighting with particular weapons; applying it with any weapon makes the narrowed lists (only Rabenschnabel, only Großschild) meaningless.

### `shared` · tiered-manoeuvre-stufe

**A hero with a manoeuvre at Stufe III (Finte, Wuchtschlag): may they announce it at a lower Stufe for a single attack?**

Both pages say "pro Stufe der Sonderfertigkeit" without saying whether that is the owned Stufe or a chosen one. The app offers every Wuchtschlag Stufe up to the owned one, but only the highest Finte Stufe (CombatAttackViews.availableManeuvers).

Situations: finte 8.3, wuchtschlag 9.3

- **a** Any Stufe up to the owned one, chosen per attack. *App:* Finte gains Stufe I/II rows like Wuchtschlag — *recommended*
- **b** Always the owned Stufe. *App:* Wuchtschlag loses its lower rows

Why a: the trade (AT for effect) is the decision the manoeuvre offers; forcing the top Stufe makes a higher Stufe a handicap against strong defenders

### `shared` · sf-technique-lists

Answer in [`rules/rulings.yaml:78`](./rules/rulings.yaml#L78)

**Every combat SF and manoeuvre lists "Kampftechniken" (Finte, Wuchtschlag, Sturmangriff, Unterlaufen, Beidhändiger Kampf, Verteidigungshaltung, …). May its effect be used only while the hero fights with a weapon of a listed technique, or does the list only say who may learn it?**

The general Kampfsonderfertigkeiten page on the wiki does not say. The app checks no technique for any SF or manoeuvre today; the catalog note on SA_67 says so on purpose until the loadout can name Raufen (issue #14). The lists are specific (Unterlaufen leaves out Stangenwaffen, whose reach it would never need; Finte's page adds techniques from later books, which is pointless if the list did not restrict use). Kampfstile ask the same question separately (kampfstil-techniques).

Situations: finte 8.5, wuchtschlag 9.5, sturmangriff 10.2, reichweite RW.7, beidhaendiger-kampf 11.4

- **a** Only with a listed technique; Raufen means unarmed. Not offered otherwise. *App:* each SF file carries `techniques:`, the offer checks the weapon in hand — *recommended*
- **b** The list only says who can learn it; usable with any weapon. *App:* no change
- **c** The same answer as kampfstil-techniques. *App:* follows that ruling

Why a: the lists are specific enough that reading them as learning lists only makes them meaningless
