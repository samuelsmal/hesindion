### `reiterkampf` · mounted-attack-side

Answer in [`rules/core/reiterkampf.yaml:286`](./rules/core/reiterkampf.yaml#L286)

**RK5: mounted, a shield blocks only attacks from the front and the shield-arm side; an attack from the weapon-arm side can only be parried with the weapon or dodged. How does the app learn which side an attack on a mounted hero comes from?**

Raised by the owner when answering angriff-von-hinten.mounted-from-behind-shield: "when mounted the player is not guided to determine from which side the attack is occurring". The app asks one side question, "von hinten" (the gm.fact fromBehind on the defence screen, CombatDefenseSetupView), on foot and mounted alike; from behind, mounted, the shield parry and its passive bonus are now gone (decided). The weapon-arm side is never asked, so the shield parry is offered against it (RK5 is shown as text only). On foot no side but behind matters. The side is the GM's call, per attack, like "von hinten". Which arm is the shield arm is the hero's: the loadout knows the shield hand.

Situations: kampfsituationen 17.10

- **a** Mounted, the defence screen replaces the 'von hinten' toggle with one side question: vorne / Schildarm / Waffenarm / hinten, default vorne. *App:* one picker on the mounted defence screen; Waffenarm drops the shield parry (RK5), hinten also its passive bonus and adds −4 (AH1) — *recommended*
- **b** Keep 'von hinten' and add a second toggle 'von der Waffenarmseite' on the mounted defence screen. *App:* one more toggle; the two exclude each other
- **c** Text only: the shield parry reminds that it is not allowed from the weapon-arm side. *App:* a reminder line on the mounted shield parry

Why a: the four sides are one fact with four values, and a picker asks it once and cannot be set contradictorily; defaulting to vorne keeps the common case one tap, and the rule's effect becomes a named line instead of a reminder the player must apply in their head
