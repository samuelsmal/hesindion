### `reiterkampf` · rider-ini

Answer in [`rules/core/reiterkampf.yaml:196`](./rules/core/reiterkampf.yaml#L196)

**The rider rolls initiative from the mount's INI base (RK1). Does the rider's own Belastung still come off it?**

RK1 swaps the base value and says nothing else. Belastung's page takes INI off per Stufe (COND_1.B3), and RK7's relief covers Kampfproben only; INI is not a check. The app uses the mount's base and takes nothing off (CombatSetupViews.mountBaseINI). A hero in plate on foot has INI −3; on the horse, today, 0.

Situations: belastung 3.1, reiterkampf 5.17

- **a** The mount's INI base as it is — the horse moves, not the rider. *App:* no change — *recommended*
- **b** The mount's INI base minus the rider's full Belastung. *App:* −3 in plate
- **c** The mount's INI base minus the rider's Belastung, relieved by RK7 as on a Kampfprobe. *App:* −2 in plate

Why a: RK1 replaces the value the INI roll starts from, and the replacement is the horse's; the page gives no hint that the rider's gear reaches it.

### `reiterkampf` · passierschlag-on-mount

Answer in [`rules/core/reiterkampf.yaml:233`](./rules/core/reiterkampf.yaml#L233)

**RK11: a foot fighter's Passierschlag hits the mount, never the rider. What should the app do when a mounted hero takes a Passierschlag (fleeing, RK15; a failed Sturmangriff)?**

The app tracks the mount's LE (Pet.currentLifeEnergy, MountHealingSheet) but the take-damage screen only ever damages the hero. RK10 then asks for a Reiten check when the mount takes SP. Opponents themselves are not modelled (ADR-0005), but the damage lands on our side of the table.

- **a** Text only: the take-damage screen reminds that the mount is the target. *App:* a reminder line
- **b** The take-damage screen offers 'Reittier getroffen': SP go to the mount's LE, then RK10's Reiten check. *App:* a toggle, a second target, the RK10 check — *recommended*

Why b: the app already keeps the mount's LE, so the damage has somewhere to go and RK10 follows from it

### `shared` · kampfstil-techniques

Answer in [`rules/rulings.yaml:16`](./rules/rulings.yaml#L16)

**A Kampfstil lists combat techniques, sometimes narrowed to weapons. Does the style's effect apply only while fighting with one of them?**

Golgariten-Stil lists Hiebwaffen (only Rabenschnabel) and Schilde (only Großschild, only mounted). The style's own page does not say "only while fighting with", and the wiki's Kampfstil overview has no general rule text. The catalog today turns the style on only with a Rabenschnabel or a Großschild in hand, so a Golgarite with a Schwert gets AT +2, PA +2 instead of AT +4, PA +3. The same question comes up for every Kampfstil.

Situations: reiterkampf 5.7

- **a** Only while the hero fights with a listed technique (and weapon, where narrowed). *App:* as the catalog does today — *recommended*
- **b** Whenever the style's own conditions hold; the list only says which techniques can learn it. *App:* the style applies with any weapon
- **c** Decided per style, in each style's file. *App:* a field per Kampfstil

Why a: A style is a way of fighting with particular weapons; applying it with any weapon makes the narrowed lists (only Rabenschnabel, only Großschild) meaningless.
