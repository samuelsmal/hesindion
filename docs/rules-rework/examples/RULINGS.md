### `SA_74` · vp-sequence

Answer in [`rules/abilities/SA_74.yaml:105`](./rules/abilities/SA_74.yaml#L105)

**When is the Selbstbeherrschung check rolled — before the spell check, or after it, when the LeP are paid? And does a caster whose spell check failed need it to pay part of the half cost with LeP?**

The page puts the pain at paying ("Dies ist ausgesprochen schmerzhaft"), and paying is step 5 of the Spruchzauberei page, after the check (step 4). But "Misslingt diese, misslingt auch der Zauber" reads as if the spell's outcome were still open. VP3 then says the half cost is paid AsP first, then LE — with no word on a check. The app has no Verbotene Pforten (catalog SA_74 todo) and charges no AsP at all (zaubermodifikationen.ZM12).

Situations: probe-magie 20.7, probe-magie 20.8

- **a** After: roll the spell; if it succeeds and LeP are to be paid, roll Selbstbeherrschung. Failing it turns the success into a failure (half cost, AsP first). A failed spell check pays its half cost as VP3 says, AsP first then LE, with no Selbstbeherrschung. *App:* the LeP choice is made before the roll; the Selbstbeherrschung check opens after a successful spell check; the result screen can still turn to 'misslungen'
- **b** Before: announce the LeP, roll Selbstbeherrschung first; on a failure the spell fails without its own check (half cost, AsP first). On a success roll the spell as usual. *App:* Selbstbeherrschung is rolled first, as a gate; the spell check only opens after it — *recommended*

Why b: the caster commits to the LeP before casting, and "misslingt auch der Zauber" is simplest as a gate before the spell check, the way reiterkampf's orders put the Reiten check before the order; it avoids a result that changes after it is shown, and needs no new concept of one check overturning another

### `zaubermodifikationen` · cost-off-table

Answer in [`rules/core/zaubermodifikationen.yaml:323`](./rules/core/zaubermodifikationen.yaml#L323)

**Erzwingen and Kosten senken move the cost "um eine Stufe" on ZM8's table (1, 2, 4, … 32 AsP). What do they do to a cost that is not on the table, or not one number?**

Of 445 spells in rules.db, many costs are on the table ("4 AsP", "8 AsP", "16 AsP"), but others are not: a choice of amounts (Armatrutz "4/8/16 AsP" for RS 1/2/3), a rate (Balsam Salabunde "1 AsP pro LeP, mindestens 4"), a floor ("4+ AsP"), or a value between steps. Situations: probe-magie 20.6

- **a** A step is a factor of two, for any amount: Erzwingen doubles, Kosten senken halves (rounded up, at least 1 AsP). For a choice (4/8/16) the step applies to the chosen amount; for a rate, to the total. *App:* one rule for every cost; the cost line shows 'Kosten senken: 8 → 4' — *recommended*
- **b** Only costs on the table can be modified; for the others the two modifications are not offered. *App:* the picker disables Erzwingen and Kosten senken with 'Kosten nicht in der Tabelle'
- **c** A step is to the neighbouring table value: 3 AsP → 4 or 2; a choice or a rate is the GM's call. *App:* table lookup with rounding to the nearest step; a GM field for the rest

Why a: the table is powers of two, so "one Stufe" and "×2 / ÷2" agree wherever the table reaches; extending it by the same factor gives every spell one answer the player can check, and rounding up with a floor of 1 AsP follows round-up and ZM5's "nie unter 1 AsP"
